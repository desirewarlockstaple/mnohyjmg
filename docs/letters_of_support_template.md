# Letters of support — template

For MIT Solve, GEEP, RELX and Zayed Sustainability Prize submissions, jurors
expect 1–3 letters of support (LoS) from external stakeholders. Below are
ready-to-customise templates. Send via personal email; ask the signer to
forward the signed PDF back.

## A. School principal (Global High Schools category)

```
[Date]

To the [Prize name] selection committee,

It is my pleasure to support the application of the TideGuard AI team
from [school name].

Over the past [N] months, [founder name] and the team have engaged
[N] of our students in the TideGuard Environmental Education module
(10 lessons) and have led [N] organised cleanups along [coastline],
removing [N] kg of plastic. Their work has measurably improved
student engagement in our science curriculum and has motivated several
students to begin their own conservation projects.

[School name] is proud to be the anchor pilot school for TideGuard
and will continue to support the project for at least the next academic
year.

Yours sincerely,
[Name]
Principal, [school name]
[email] | [phone]
```

## B. NGO / community partner

```
[Date]

To the [Prize name] selection committee,

[Organisation] supports the TideGuard AI team's submission.

We are an NGO working on [marine conservation / coastal cleanups / youth
education] in [region]. We have been collaborating with TideGuard since
[date] and have found their hot-spot prediction tool [genuinely useful /
promising / unique] in our planning. In particular, [one specific story
of value delivered — e.g. "we redirected our March cleanup to the bay
TideGuard flagged and recovered 2x the usual mass"].

We are committed to continuing this collaboration and to providing
TideGuard with the cleanup data needed to validate and improve the
forecast model.

Sincerely,
[Name], [title]
[Organisation]
```

## C. Academic mentor (ML or marine biology)

```
[Date]

To the [Prize name] selection committee,

I am writing in strong support of the TideGuard AI project.

I am a [position] at [university / lab] working on [marine biology /
machine learning for Earth observation]. I have reviewed TideGuard's
codebase, model card and research note. The use of a Physics-Informed
Neural Network for short-range marine debris transport is technically
sound; the team's choice to learn the windage, diffusion and beaching
coefficients directly from data is a useful contribution. Their
benchmark against persistence and Lagrangian baselines is rigorous and
matches standard practice in the field.

For a [grade-level] team this is exceptional work, and I am happy to
provide further academic mentorship as the project develops.

Yours,
[Name]
[Title], [Institution]
[email]
```

## D. Youth-program coordinator

```
[Date]

To the [Prize name] selection committee,

I run [program name], a [description] that works with [N] youth in
[region]. [Founder name] and the TideGuard team have integrated the EE
module into our after-school programming since [date], reaching [N]
participants in [N] sessions.

The lessons are well-pitched for our audience, the practical tasks are
genuinely actionable, and the gamification (XP, badges, certificate)
materially improves attendance and completion. I recommend TideGuard
without reservation.

[Name]
[Title], [program name]
```

---

## How to ask

- Personalise the email — never paste the template raw.
- Attach: a 1-page project summary (use `docs/proposal.md` section 1–3).
- Ask for the letter at least **3 weeks** before the submission deadline.
- Offer to draft a first version they edit — most signers prefer this.
