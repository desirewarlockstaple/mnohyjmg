# TideGuard — Teacher Guide

A 12-page guide for educators delivering the TideGuard EE module. Aligned with
Taiwan 108 Curriculum, Cambridge IGCSE Environmental Management,
AP Environmental Science, IB ESS and Singapore O-level Geography. Free under
CC-BY-4.0.

## Who this is for

- Middle-school and high-school teachers (grades 7–12).
- Youth-program facilitators at NGOs, libraries, after-school clubs.
- University outreach volunteers supporting community-science programs.

You do **not** need a science degree to deliver TideGuard. Each lesson is
self-contained and ends with a five-question quiz and a practical task you
can run with a class of 20 in 45 minutes.

## Suggested 10-week unit

| Week | Lesson | In-class activity | Homework | Assessment |
|-----:|--------|-------------------|----------|------------|
| 1 | 01 marine-plastic | Brainstorm: how do bottles cross oceans? | Read lesson + quiz | Quiz score |
| 2 | 02 lifecycle | Bottle-to-ocean trace exercise | Submit one report via app | App report |
| 3 | 03 microplastic | Filter tap water + observe | Read lesson + quiz | Lab notes |
| 4 | 04 read-the-map | Open `/map` and identify 1 hotspot | Screenshot + 100-word interp | Map screenshot |
| 5 | 05 good-report | Submit a high-quality report | Submit two more reports | Reports approved by moderator |
| 6 | 06 safety | Pack a cleanup safety kit (gloves, bags, scale, first-aid) | Read lesson + quiz | Kit check |
| 7 | 07 organize-cleanup | Plan an event with one classmate | Confirm one date + venue | Event-plan one-pager |
| 8 | 08 sort-waste | Sort a sample haul into 5 streams | Read lesson + quiz | Sorting accuracy |
| 9 | 09 reduce-reuse | Eliminate one single-use item from your week | Reflection one-pager | Reflection |
| 10 | 10 lead-school | Recruit two classmates for the next cohort | Sign 2-person commit form | Recruitment proof |

At the end of week 10, students earning ≥80 % across all quizzes
receive a TideGuard PDF certificate generated by the API
(`GET /education/certificate`).

## Classroom-management notes

- **Photo consent**: never photograph a student's face in a TideGuard
  submission. Use the back-camera with the student in the frame from
  behind, or use objects only. EXIF GPS is stripped automatically at
  upload by the API.
- **Severity ratings**: encourage students to under-report rather than
  over-report. The moderator queue handles ambiguity; over-reporting
  pollutes the training set.
- **Mobile access**: the lessons render on the mobile app and on the web.
  If your classroom has shared tablets, instruct students to log out
  after each session.
- **Offline learning**: lessons are cached in `shared_preferences` on
  first open. Students without home internet can still complete the
  course at school and sync next time they connect.

## Safety checklist for the cleanup events

- Adult-to-student ratio ≥ 1 : 8.
- Gloves and closed-toe shoes mandatory.
- First-aid kit on-site.
- Tide-tables checked the morning of the event.
- Weather contingency: if the forecast is severe, run a sorting workshop
  indoors instead.
- Recording: total mass, GPS polygon (we provide a Flutter widget that
  records this in three taps).

## Common questions

**Q: I'm not technical. Can I still teach the EE module?**
Yes. Every lesson is plain-language Markdown; the quizzes auto-grade; the
certificates auto-generate. You need a smartphone and a browser.

**Q: What if my school can't approve photo collection?**
Use the lessons without the reporting component. The 10 quizzes still
qualify students for the certificate. Discuss prediction-only with
classes that cannot collect data.

**Q: Can students contribute code?**
Yes. The repo is MIT-licensed. We tag good first issues with
`good-first-issue` and pair students with mentors via Discord. See
`CONTRIBUTING.md`.
